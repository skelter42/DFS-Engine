# 2026-09-29 — Plus odds formula

- Sport: all
- Status: validated (user correction)
- Durable lesson: a plus price is `100 / (odds + 100)`. +155 is 39%. Using `odds / (odds + 100)` on a plus price flips it and invents goals. A one-sided price still converts. A goal price is the goal piece, not the whole player, until the shot line is added.
