# Vegas Consensus — the projection

Do this every time. The first action is the prop board. Vegas is the main engine.

## Who matters

The projection that matters is the posted 1–9 and the pitcher. On a bullpen game that is the opener and the bulk. That is the coverage universe.

Do not chase the whole Savant file. Bench bats and extra arms are not the grade. Savant 0 stays 0.

## Order

1. Prop boards across DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, and every other book that posts the market.
2. Each price is a percentage. +150 is 40%. Strip the vig inside that book so the two sides sum to 1. Median the books.
3. Convert the consensus into site points. That is the projection uploaded to Savant.
4. No prop after the sweep: industry consensus, the median of DFS-site projections.
5. No site number either: Savant, unchanged.

A two-sided prop is a Vegas projection. A hits price converts. Sweep the game board before anyone in the 1–9 falls through.

The blend is across sportsbooks. A DFS site does not get averaged into a priced player.

## Board

Loop games, not names. One event page covers the game. Pull the pitcher family and the hitter family for the posted order.

DraftKings points, when the component is posted: inning 2.25, strikeout 2, win 4, earned run −2, hit allowed −0.6, walk allowed −0.6. Single 3, double 5, triple 8, home run 10, RBI 2, run 2, walk 2, stolen base 5. A hit price becomes expected hits, then those points.

Do not invent a prop from a team total.

## Required mix

Every output reports two mixes.

On the posted 1–9 and the pitcher:

- n Vegas (x%)
- n industry, no prop (x%)
- n Savant fallback (x%)

On the rest of the positive file, the same three counts, separate. Zeros are not in either percentage.

A is Vegas on the starters and the posted order. The fringe does not lower the grade.
