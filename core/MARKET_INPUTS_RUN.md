# Market Inputs — Run Card

Read this, then `core/VEGAS_CONSENSUS.md`. Do not read a retired card for the estimator.

## Order

1. Intake the attached file. Keep Name, DFS ID, row order, Own.
2. Savant 0 stays 0. Do not research those rows.
3. For each game, hit the prop boards. DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, and the other books. Both sides.
4. De-vig inside each book. Median the books. Convert that consensus into site points. That is Proj.
5. No two-sided prop after the sweep: DFS-site median.
6. No site number either: leave Savant unchanged.
7. Write the file. Stop. Do not build lineups.

## Required mix

Every output states the mix on the positive-Proj pool, as counts and percentages:

- Vegas
- Industry, no prop
- Savant fallback

Zeros are reported separately and are not in the percentage.

## Grade

- A+: DraftKings, FanDuel, and BetMGM both-side prices on the starters and the posted order.
- A: multi-book props on the starters. Industry only where every book skipped a name.
- B: one book, one side, or a starter with no prop.
