# NCAAF DFS Engine

## Core Philosophy
College football DFS is driven by role, play volume, scoring environment, and correlation. Depth-chart uncertainty and blowout risk require more explicit game-script coverage than a pure median projection build.

## Required Checks
- starting QB and skill-position roles
- injuries / depth-chart changes
- spreads, totals, and implied team totals
- pace / play volume
- target and rushing concentration
- red-zone role
- projected ownership
- weather where material

## Correlation
Prioritize coherent combinations such as QB with one or more pass catchers, game stacks in competitive high-total environments, and bring-backs when the opposing offense is capable of forcing continued aggression.

Avoid assuming every high-total game must be stacked. Compare field ownership with each game's plausible ceiling and failure modes.

## Portfolio Game Scripts
Across a multi-lineup set, intentionally represent outcomes such as:
- favorite controls game through rushing volume
- favorite scores through passing instead of rushing
- underdog keeps game competitive and forces volume
- shootout exceeds market total
- popular game disappoints
- low-owned high-tempo game becomes the slate winner

## Exposure Audit
Every delivered lineup set must include Sim Savant/source projected ownership vs DFS Engine final exposure and percentage-point difference. Major deviations require a stated role, leverage, ceiling, or correlation reason.
