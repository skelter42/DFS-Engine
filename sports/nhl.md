# NHL DFS Engine

## Core Philosophy
NHL DFS is high variance and strongly correlation-driven. Line stacks, power-play correlation, shot volume, goalie context, and ownership should be considered together.

## Required Checks
- confirmed/expected line combinations
- power-play units
- starting goalies
- injuries / scratches
- team totals and game totals
- shot-generation environment
- projected ownership

## Portfolio Construction
Prefer correlated skater groups over isolated median projections. Diversify across plausible scoring environments while maintaining enough concentration in selected teams and lines to capture ceiling.

NHL naturally supports broader player pools across a portfolio than lower-variance sports; do not mistake diversification for randomness.

## Exposure Audit
Every final lineup set must include source projected ownership vs DFS Engine exposure with percentage-point difference and reasons for major deviations.
